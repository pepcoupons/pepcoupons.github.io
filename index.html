Frequently asked questions

How do I use a peptide vendor coupon code?

Copy the code, open the vendor's store, add products to your cart and paste the code into the discount field at checkout. Check that a discount line appears before you pay.

Why did a coupon code not work?

Codes can expire, apply only to some products or need a minimum order. Check the terms on the vendor's site, and use the last-checked date on each listing to see how recent the code is.

Are the codes on PepCoupons verified?

Each listing shows the date it was last checked. Listings marked as demo are placeholders and are not active offers.

Does PepCoupons sell peptides or give medical advice?

No. PepCoupons only lists discount codes from vendors. It does not sell products and nothing on the site is medical advice.
